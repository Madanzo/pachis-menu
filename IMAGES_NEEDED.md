## Image Specifications

**Recommended:**
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Size:** 500x500px (square)
- **Max file size:** Under 500KB each
- **Background:** Clean, professional, ideally white or transparent

**Tips:**
1. Use https://tinypng.com to compress images before uploading
2. Keep filenames lowercase with dashes (no spaces)
3. Make sure images are clear and show the product well
4. Square aspect ratio works best for the grid layout

Once you add images to the `images/` folder, they'll automatically appear on your site!
